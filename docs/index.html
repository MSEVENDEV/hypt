Features

Everything a clipper should be.
Nothing it shouldn't.

Built on FFmpeg's GPU capture and your graphics card's video engine. No account, no cloud, no ads.

Instant replay on the GPU

Desktop Duplication + NVENC, AMF or Quick Sync. ~0.1% CPU, no FPS drop. Clips are stitched, never re-encoded.

Offline voice engine

“Hypt, clip that.” “Clip the last two minutes.” Offline Vosk speech model tuned for gaming noise & accents. Live voice check in Settings.

Sync with NVIDIA, AMD & Medal

Save into your existing game folders (Desktop, VALORANT…) with matching names. Browse thousands of clips in seconds; Hypt never deletes other clippers' clips.

Hype detector

Get loud — relative to your normal voice — and Hypt clips the moment for you. The “LET'S GO” button you never press.

Every length & Alt+F1 screenshot

15 s, 30 s, 1, 2 or 5 minutes — each on its own shortcut. Record from the past, or press Alt+F1 for instant PNG screenshots.

Windows Media Player ready

H.264 by default so Windows' Media Player, Discord, and WhatsApp open clips instantly. HEVC and AV1 are one click away.

Clips to your phone

Scan a QR code and your clips open on your phone over Wi-Fi. PIN-protected, auto-off, no cloud, no account.

Encrypted vault

AES-256-GCM, tamper-evident, password never stored. Locked clips vanish from your folder, gallery and phone.

Privacy guard

When a password manager (or any app you choose) is on screen, that footage is destroyed from the buffer.

Perfect sync, separate tracks

Game and mic re-clocked to the video's clock — no drift, ever. Mix, game-only and mic-only tracks, mic noise removal.

In-app updates

A friendly pop-up offers Later, Schedule (when you close Hypt, or tonight at 3:00 — never mid-game) or Update now & restart.

Storage cap that respects you

Auto-cleanup recycles only Hypt's own unstarred clips to stay under the limit. NVIDIA clips and your own files are never touched.

Voice

Your hands are busy.
Your mouth isn't.

“Hypt” sounds exactly like “hyped”, so the wake word is natural to shout. Recognition runs fully offline on a built-in Vosk model tuned for accents (“Haybed, clib that”) and loud game audio. Settings includes a live voice check.

Tuned for game audio and accents: 8 commands recognised, normal conversation correctly ignored.
